Try bwtidy

bwtidy is a self-hosted web UI and MCP server for tidying a Bitwarden or Vaultwarden vault. This demo gives you a vault of your own on a server that only the demo uses, filled with sample items, and logs you in.

Your demo vault and everything in it are deleted when the session ends: after $idle without activity, after $total at most, or when you click End demo.

Never enter your own details, passwords or secrets in the demo.
